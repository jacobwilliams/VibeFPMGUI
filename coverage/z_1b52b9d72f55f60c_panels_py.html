26class RedockOnDoubleClick(QObject): 

27 """Double-clicking a floating panel's title docks it back, once the 

28 mouse button is up. 

29 

30 On macOS a floating panel has the system's title bar, and pressing on it 

31 starts a window move that macOS carries out; the second press of a 

32 double-click starts one too. 

33 Qt docked the panel at the double-click itself, in the middle of that 

34 move: the panel was left over the main window instead of in its place 

35 (and macOS logged "Window move completed without beginning"). Here the 

36 double-click is taken over, and the panel is docked (as setFloating(False) 

37 does, which works) when the move is done.""" 

38

111 def _build_docks(self, options: QWidget, files_box: QWidget, graph_tab: QWidget, console_box: QWidget): 

112 """Every panel but the editor is a dock: moved to the left, right or 

113 bottom, stacked as tabs on another, floated as a window of its own, or

116 # window of Qt's own, and once that's closed its panels come back stuck 

117 # over the editor, outside the layout. One panel at a time is solid. 

118 # Not AnimatedDocks: on macOS the panel layout could start animating 

119 # against itself, each finished animation laying out the panels again 

120 # and starting the next, at 100% CPU, the window frozen (seen with a 

121 # click in the editor). Panels snap into place instead of sliding; the 

122 # style has no animation duration either: see themes.StillStyle.)
